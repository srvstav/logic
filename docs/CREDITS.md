## Atomic coordinates

DNA model: PDB **1BNA**, Drew H.R., Wing R.M., Takano T., Broka C., Tanaka S., Itakura K., Dickerson R.E. (1981), “Structure of a B-DNA dodecamer: conformation and dynamics.” https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.78.4.2179. Dataset: https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb1BNA/pdb. Downloaded from https://files.rcsb.org/download/1BNA.pdb. X-ray diffraction resolution 1.90 Å. The bundled extract preserves the 486 ATOM records; solvent HETATM records are omitted. World coordinates use a rigid axis permutation and uniform scaling. Visual bonds are inferred, not a deposited bond table. Molecular data are from the public wwPDB archive; attribution is retained here.
